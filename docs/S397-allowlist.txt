Product:     SimSeatLock
Layer name:  XR_APILAYER_NOVENDOR_sim_seat_lock
DLL:         XR_APILAYER_NOVENDOR_sim_seat_lock.dll
What it is:  a thin Khronos OpenXR API layer that keeps the VR eyepoint
             locked to the seat while a motion platform moves.

SimSeatLock is motion compensation for sim racing and flight in VR. It
reads chassis pose from a seat-mounted IMU (one sensor today; commanded
platform pose is the next source behind the same interface) and applies

  T_view = T_cor * inv(T_rig) * inv(T_cor) * T_hmd

inside xrLocateViews. Head motion relative to the bucket is unchanged.
Platform pitch, roll, and heave are subtracted about a configured center
of rotation.

It does not read or write LMU memory, telemetry, inputs, or files under
the game install. The pose process is separate. The layer is identity
until the user arms it. SteamVR remains the OpenXR runtime
(steamxr_win64.json). No OpenComposite, no replaced openvr_api.dll.
